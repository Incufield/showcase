# Copyright and DMCA

This repository hosts links and descriptions submitted by our students. If you
believe something here infringes your copyright, tell us and we will act.

## Sending a notice

Email **dmca@incufield.com** with:

1. Your signature, electronic is fine.
2. What work you say is infringed.
3. Where the infringing material is. A direct link to the file or entry.
4. Your name, address, phone number, and email.
5. A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorized by
   the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
6. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that your notice is accurate and that
   you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner.

## What we do

We remove or disable the material, tell the student who submitted it, and give
them a copy of the notice.

## Counter notice

If your entry was removed and you believe that was a mistake, email the same
address with your contact details, the material removed, and a statement under
penalty of perjury that you believe it was removed by mistake or
misidentification. Include your consent to the jurisdiction of the federal
court where you live, or where we are located if you live outside the US.

If we receive a valid counter notice, we may restore the material in 10 to 14
business days unless the original sender files a court action.

## Repeat infringers

Students who repeatedly submit infringing work lose the right to publish here.
